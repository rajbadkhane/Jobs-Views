# Jobs View Salary Research Outreach

## Purpose

Earn relevant editorial references to Jobs View salary research without buying, exchanging, automating, or fabricating links.

## Linkable Assets

- `/salary/india-report-2026`: cited evidence review and current coverage statement.
- `/salary/methodology`: normalization, confidence, provenance, and fallback policy.
- `/salary/calculator`: evidence-based role and city calculator with unavailable states.
- Qualified role and city benchmark pages: published only when current evidence exists.

## Suitable Audiences

- University and college career cells.
- Industrial Training Institutes and vocational training organizations.
- Workforce development and employment publications.
- Employer and recruiter communities.
- Labour-market researchers and education nonprofits.
- City-specific career publications where a qualified local benchmark exists.

## Outreach Standard

1. Contact only organizations with a genuine subject connection.
2. Explain the exact source, methodology, and limitation relevant to their audience.
3. Link to the most specific useful resource, not automatically to the homepage.
4. Do not request prescribed anchor text, reciprocal links, paid dofollow placement, or bulk syndication.
5. Keep a record of contact, purpose, response, corrections requested, and publication URL.
6. Correct or withdraw any benchmark that later fails the evidence standard.

## Suggested Pitch Structure

- One sentence explaining why the recipient's students, workers, or readers may benefit.
- One sentence describing the evidence and its effective date.
- One sentence stating the limitations and methodology link.
- A direct resource URL and an offer to provide accessible data or chart formats.

## Measurement

- Relevant referring domains and pages.
- Referral visits that engage with salary methodology or jobs.
- Corrections and source feedback.
- Citations from education, research, workforce, and employer publications.
- No target based on raw link volume.

## Prohibited Practices

- Purchased or rented links.
- Automated directory submission.
- Private blog networks.
- Link exchanges at scale.
- Fake scholarships, awards, employers, partnerships, or testimonials.
- Publishing thin role-city pages only to capture search traffic.
