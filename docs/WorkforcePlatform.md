# Workforce Platform

## Mission

Jobs View now models the hiring ecosystem beyond job search: documents, identity, verification, local hiring operations, referrals, trust, and workforce readiness.

## Platform Capabilities

- Employee document vault
- Digital Jobs View profile card
- Verification platform
- Walk-in, campus, job fair, and mass hiring events
- Candidate and employer referrals
- Company verification score
- Candidate trust score
- Employer hiring dashboard widgets
- Candidate personalized home widgets
- Blue-collar role profiles
- Government job hub categories
- Employer careers site routing

## Implementation

Sprint 24 adds `workforce-platform-engine.ts` as a shared, UI-neutral contract. Existing pages can consume it without changing routing, APIs, RBAC, or design.
