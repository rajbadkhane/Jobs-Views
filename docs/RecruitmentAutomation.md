# Recruitment Automation

## Employer Automation

Jobs View automation helps recruiters operate the hiring process consistently:

- Thank-you email when a candidate applies
- Recruiter notification after shortlist
- Calendar invite after interview scheduling
- 24 hour and 1 hour interview reminders
- Rejection email after rejection
- Feedback pending task after interview
- Offer expiry reminder
- Document pending task

## Pipeline Automation

Configured stage rules can move candidates forward:

- Interview passed to final interview
- Offer accepted to hired
- Joined to employee archive placeholder

## Candidate Automation

Candidate reminders cover:

- Upcoming interviews
- Documents required
- Offer waiting
- Joining checklist
- Profile incomplete
- Recommended skills
- Missing certificates

## Search Automation

Recommendation refresh is triggered by:

- Profile updates
- Job requirement updates
- Skill updates
- Location or salary preference changes
