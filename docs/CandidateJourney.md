# Candidate Journey

## Verified Journey

1. Register through `/register`.
2. Verify email through `/verify-email`.
3. Complete profile builder through `/candidate/onboarding`.
4. Upload resume through `/candidate/resume`.
5. View recommendations through `/candidate/jobs`.
6. Apply from `/jobs/[slug]`.
7. Track interviews through `/candidate/interviews`.
8. Track offers through `/candidate/offers`.
9. Review history through `/applications`.
