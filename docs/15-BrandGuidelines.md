# 🎨 Jobs View - Brand Guidelines

This document establishes the brand identity, design principles, and voice for the Jobs View platform to ensure a consistent experience across all touchpoints.

---

## 1. Core Brand Assets

### 1.1 Brand Name
- **Written Form:** Always written as **Jobs View** (CamelCase, capital C, capital O, capital S).
- **Pronunciation:** "Career O-S".

### 1.2 Logo Concept
The Jobs View logo represents upward career progression, structure, and openness.
- **Iconography:** A stylized terminal prompt symbol (`>`) combined with an upward-pointing arrow inside a sleek square bracket. This represents the developer-first mindset and the "operating system" nature of the platform.
- **Wordmark:** "Career" in Outfit Light (weight 300), "OS" in Outfit ExtraBold (weight 800) in Electric Indigo.
- **Favicon:** The minimalist `>_` symbol inside a rounded indigo tile.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Typography

### 2.1 Primary Brand Colors
- **Electric Indigo (`#4f46e5` / `hsl(243, 75%, 59%)`):** Represents innovation, ambition, and the digital ecosystem.
- **Vibrant Teal (`#14b8a6` / `hsl(172, 66%, 50%)`):** Represents growth, freshness, and success. Used for primary accents and highlights.

### 2.2 Typography
- **Headings:** **Outfit** (Sans-serif)
  - Values: Professional, modern, structured.
  - Weights: Light (300) for secondary headers, SemiBold (600) and Bold (700) for primary titles.
- **Body & Controls:** **Inter** (Sans-serif)
  - Values: High readability, neutral, technical.
  - Weights: Regular (400) for body, Medium (500) for UI labels, SemiBold (600) for buttons.

---

## 3. Design Language & Illustration Style

- **Glassmorphism:** Surfaces should use semi-transparent backgrounds with a subtle blur effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and 1px borders with low opacity to create depth.
- **Shadows:** Use soft, diffused shadows instead of hard borders (e.g., `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`).
- **Icons:** We use **Lucide Icons** exclusively.
  - *Rules:* 2px stroke width, rounded caps, and consistent sizing (20px for standard UI buttons, 24px for section headers).
- **Illustrations:** Keep illustrations abstract, geometric, and vector-based. Avoid stock photos of people in business suits. Instead, use clean UI mockups, glowing code snippets, and flow diagrams.

---

## 4. Voice & Tone

The Jobs View voice is **Direct, Transparent, and Professional**.

| Principle | We Are | We Are Not |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clarity** | Direct and helpful. | Wordy or vague. |
| **Trust** | Objective, verified, and honest. | Salesy or hyperbolic. |
| **Empowerment** | Encouraging and utility-focused. | Patronizing or overly casual. |

### Copywriting Examples:
- **Incorrect (Opaque):** *"Your application is being processed by our talent acquisition partners."*
- **Correct (Direct):** *"Acme Corp viewed your resume. Your status has been updated to Reviewed."*
- **Incorrect (Vague):** *"We have matching opportunities for you!"*
- **Correct (Specific):** *"3 new Senior Go roles matching your skills were posted today."*
