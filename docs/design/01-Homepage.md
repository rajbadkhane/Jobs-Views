# 01 - Homepage

## Goal

Redesign only the Jobs View homepage. Preserve existing homepage content model, route, search intent, public navigation, SEO behavior, and CTAs.

## Homepage Goals

- Establish Jobs View as a Career Operating System.
- Make job search immediately available.
- Build trust for candidates and employers.
- Surface trending jobs, companies, categories, and career guides.
- Provide clear candidate and employer paths.

## Navbar

Use a sticky public navbar with logo, primary links, search affordance, auth actions, theme toggle, and mobile drawer. Keep it clean and premium.

## Hero

The hero must show Jobs View identity in the first viewport. Use a search-first layout with a clear headline, subheading, trust stats, popular searches, and one meaningful visual asset.

## Search

Include keyword, location, experience, salary, remote/work mode, and search action. Inputs must be large, accessible, and mobile-friendly.

## Trending Jobs

Show compact cards with role, location, company count, and link. Use responsive grid.

## Companies

Show verified company cards with logo, name, badge, open jobs, location, and industry.

## Categories

Use clear category tiles: IT, Government, Healthcare, Finance, Marketing, Sales, Engineering, Education, Remote, Internship, Walk-in.

## Career Guides

Show article/guide cards with title, category, short summary, and read action.

## Testimonials

Use restrained quote cards. Do not over-decorate.

## CTA

Include candidate CTA and employer CTA. CTAs must point to existing routes.

## Footer

Include SEO links, popular cities, skills, companies, career resources, support, and legal links.

## Loading States

Use skeleton cards for jobs, companies, categories, and guides.

## Empty States

Show useful text and fallback links if sections have no content.

## Responsive

Mobile: hero search stacks, nav becomes drawer, cards become single column. Tablet: two-column sections. Desktop: strong grid.

## Animation

Use subtle fade/translate on sections, hover lift on cards, and no delayed usability.

## Accessibility

Use semantic sections, one H1, labeled inputs, keyboard navigation, and accessible buttons.

## Prompt For Stitch

Read `00-DesignSystem.md` then redesign ONLY Homepage. Preserve all existing homepage routes, content intent, navigation, search behavior, SEO structure, and CTAs. Redesign only UI/UX.
