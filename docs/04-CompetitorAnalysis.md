# 📊 Jobs View - Competitor Analysis

To succeed in the crowded recruitment space, Jobs View must identify the strengths and weaknesses of existing platforms and capitalize on their shortcomings.

---

## 1. Competitive Landscape Overview

We analyze four major competitors across the recruitment spectrum:

1. **LinkedIn:** The dominant professional network and job board.
2. **Indeed:** The largest aggregator of job postings.
3. **Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent):** The go-to platform for startups and tech roles.
4. **Traditional ATS Platforms (e.g., Greenhouse, Lever):** Internal software used by employers (not public job boards, but competitors for employer workflows).

## 3. Deep-Dive Competitor Analysis

### LinkedIn
- **Strengths:** Unrivaled network size, rich user data, powerful search.
- **Weaknesses:** Highly bloated UI, filled with irrelevant social media feed noise ("LinkedIn Lunatics"), expensive premium subscriptions, and high volumes of ghost/expired jobs. Candidates feel their applications enter a black hole.
- **Jobs View Opportunity:** Create a utility-first, noise-free platform focused purely on job seeking and hiring, removing the social feed entirely.

### Indeed
- **Strengths:** Massive volume of jobs, high traffic, simple application (Indeed Apply).
- **Weaknesses:** Extremely outdated interface, high rate of spam and scam listings, poor candidate experience, and lack of transparency.
- **Jobs View Opportunity:** Deliver a premium, high-trust experience with a highly curated selection of verified jobs and a beautiful modern interface.

### Wellfound (AngelList)
- **Strengths:** Excellent for startups, high salary transparency, direct connection with founders.
- **Weaknesses:** Niche focus (tech/startups only), limited tools for larger-scale recruitment, and can feel disorganized as companies grow.
- **Jobs View Opportunity:** Combine the salary transparency and startup-friendly nature of Wellfound with a robust, scalable ATS capability that appeals to both startups and established mid-sized businesses.

### Greenhouse / Lever
- **Strengths:** Deep integration into corporate workflows, excellent candidate pipeline management.
- **Weaknesses:** Extremely expensive, difficult to set up, and offers a terrible candidate application experience (requires candidates to create an account for every company).
- **Jobs View Opportunity:** Build a lightweight, beautiful ATS directly into the platform that requires no setup and provides a seamless, unified application experience for candidates.
