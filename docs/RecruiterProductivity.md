# Recruiter Productivity

## Metrics

Jobs View tracks recruiter and company hiring performance through:

- Time to hire
- Stage drop-off
- Bottleneck stage
- Interview completion rate
- Feedback completion rate
- Offer acceptance rate
- Joining rate
- Recruiter task completion
- Candidate response follow-up rate

## Task Engine

Recruiter task types:

- Today's interviews
- Follow-ups
- Pending reviews
- Offer expiry
- Documents
- Background check
- Overdue items

## Reporting

Reports should be grouped by:

- Company
- Job
- Recruiter
- Department
- Date range
- Workflow template
