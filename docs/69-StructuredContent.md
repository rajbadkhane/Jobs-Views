# 📄 Jobs View - Structured Content Templates

This document defines the layout grids, mandatory sections, and component templates for the primary content pages on Jobs View.

---

## 1. Job Posting Page Template

Every job detail page must follow a structured, 3-column layout on desktop:

- **Column 1 (Main Content - 65% Width):**
  - **Job Title & Company Name:** Large Outfit heading.
  - **Quick Specs Badge Bar:** Inline badges for Location, Job Type, Salary, and Experience.
  - **About the Role:** Markdown text.
  - **Requirements:** Bulleted list.
  - **Benefits:** Bulleted list.
- **Column 2 (Sidebar Actions - 35% Width):**
  - **Apply Card:** Prominent "Apply Now" button, primary resume selector, and cover letter field.
  - **Company Mini-Card:** Logo, website link, size, and responsiveness score.
- **Under the Fold:**
  - **Similar Jobs Widget:** 3 related job cards.
  - **FAQ Accordion:** Localized role Q&As.

---

## 2. Company Profile Page Template

- **Hero Banner:** Company cover image (or gradient), logo, company name, and verification badge.
- **Tabbed Navigation:**
  - **About:** Mission, size, industry, location, and social links.
  - **Jobs:** List of active job postings (rendered as card grid).
  - **Culture & Benefits:** Text and image grid of team culture.
- **Responsiveness Indicator:** A prominent widget displaying average response time (e.g., *"Typically reviews applications in 2 days"*).

---

## 3. Salary Guide Page Template

- **Hero Header:** H1: *"Average {Role} Salary in {Location}"*.
- **Salary Widget:** Large text displaying the median salary, accompanied by a visual slider showing the 10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles.
- **Data Table:** Comparison of average salaries across different experience levels (Junior, Mid, Senior) and cities.
- **Call to Action:** *"Browse {Role} Jobs in {Location}"* button.
- **FAQ:** Standard Q&As regarding salary distributions and top-paying companies.
