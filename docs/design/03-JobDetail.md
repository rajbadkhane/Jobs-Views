# 03 - Job Detail

## Goal

Redesign only the job detail page. Preserve apply, save, share, SEO metadata, Google Jobs structured data, and related jobs behavior.

## Hero

Show title, company, location, work mode, job type, salary, posted date, and primary actions.

## Apply Card

Desktop apply card may sit in sticky sidebar. Mobile apply action should become sticky bottom action.

## Sticky Sidebar

Include apply, save, share, job summary, company summary, and report action if present.

## Company Card

Show logo, company name, verification badge, industry, location, open jobs, and link to company page.

## Salary Card

Show salary range, currency, compensation type, and note if salary is not provided.

## Benefits

Use badge/grid layout. Keep content readable.

## Requirements

Use structured sections with bullets and clear headings.

## Skills

Show required and preferred skills separately where data exists.

## Related Jobs and Similar Jobs

Use compact job cards with clear links.

## SEO Blocks

Include breadcrumb, FAQ, structured content, and internal links.

## Responsive

Mobile: single column, sticky apply button. Desktop: content plus sidebar.

## Animation

Subtle entry and hover only.

## Prompt For Stitch

Read `00-DesignSystem.md` then redesign ONLY Job Detail. Preserve existing application, save/share, SEO, and structured data workflows.
