# 📝 Jobs View - Content Strategy & Pillars

This document defines the content pillars and publishing strategy for Jobs View to build topical authority and drive organic candidate acquisition.

---

## 1. Core Content Pillars

We organize our content into 9 distinct pillars, designed to capture candidates at every stage of their career journey:

```text
├── 1. Jobs                 - Live job listings (transactional pages)
├── 2. Career               - Career path guides (e.g., "How to become a Go developer")
├── 3. Education            - Recommended courses, certifications, and skills mappings
├── 4. Guidance             - Resume writing tips, portfolio reviews, and career advice
├── 5. Companies            - In-depth company profiles and culture insights
├── 6. Salary               - Location-specific and experience-based salary calculators
├── 7. Interview            - Role-specific interview questions and answers
├── 8. News                 - Tech hiring trends, layoffs, and funding updates
└── 9. Blogs                - Thought leadership and industry insights
```

---

## 2. Pillar Details & Intent Matching

### 2.1 Jobs (High Transactional Intent)
- **Content:** Live, verified job descriptions.
- **Goal:** Drive direct applications.
- **SEO Strategy:** Google Jobs schema, localized role keywords.

### 2.2 Career Guides (High Informational Intent)
- **Content:** Step-by-step guides mapping out career progressions.
- **Goal:** Capture top-of-funnel traffic (students and career changers).
- **SEO Strategy:** Comprehensive FAQ sections, structured how-to schemas.

### 2.3 Salary Insights (High Informational Intent)
- **Content:** Interactive salary graphs, average ranges, and city-by-city comparisons.
- **Goal:** Drive viral sharing and capture high-intent salary search queries.
- **SEO Strategy:** Clean tabular data, dynamic localized titles (e.g., *"React Developer Salary in India"*).

---

## 3. Interlocking Pillar Architecture

To build topical authority, pillars must not exist in isolation. They are interlinked to form a content web:
- A **Career Guide** (e.g., *"How to become a React Developer"*) links to the **Salary Page** (*"React Developer Salary"*), which links to **Active Jobs** (*"React Jobs in Bhopal"*), which links to **Company Profiles** (*"Acme Corp"*).
- This structure distributes link equity (PageRank) and guides users naturally down the funnel from reading a guide to applying for a job.
