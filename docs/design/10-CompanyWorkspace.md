# 10 - Company Workspace

## Goal

Redesign only company workspace. Preserve company CRUD, media, verification, branches, departments, documents, and settings workflows.

## Company Overview

Show logo, banner, name, status, industry, size, headquarters, website, about, mission, vision, culture.

## Gallery

Show gallery grid, upload state, delete state, and empty state.

## Documents

Show GST, CIN, verification documents, upload and status.

## Branches

List branches with city, state, country, address, map link if available.

## Departments

Show department list and actions.

## Verification

Show verification checklist and status.

## Media

Upload logo, banner, gallery, documents with progress.

## Responsive

Mobile uses stacked cards and sticky action buttons.
