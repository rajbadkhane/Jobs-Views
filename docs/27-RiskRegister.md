# ⚠️ Jobs View - Risk Register & Mitigation Strategy

This document identifies the primary technical, operational, and product risks associated with the Jobs View platform, along with their impact, probability, and mitigation plans.

---

## 1. Risk Matrix

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Scope Creep** | High | Medium | Strictly adhere to the [Day 1 Feature List](file:///d:/New%20folder/Jobs%20View/docs/06-Features.md). Defer additional ideas (e.g., AI resume rewriting, automated email follow-ups) to Phase 2. |
| **Performance Degradation** | Medium | High | Implement [Redis Caching](file:///d:/New%20folder/Jobs%20View/docs/24-Performance.md#L28), database indexing on search columns, and utilize Next.js SSR with edge caching. |
| **Security Breaches (XSS/SQLi)**| Low | High | Use [SQLc Parameterized Queries](file:///d:/New%20folder/Jobs%20View/docs/22-Security.md#L30) to block SQL injection. Enforce `httpOnly`, `Secure` cookies for JWTs to prevent XSS session hijacking. |
| **Fake Job / Spam Postings** | High | High | Require **mandatory domain verification** for employers. An employer registering with `gmail.com` cannot post jobs; they must use a verified business domain (e.g., `acme.com`). |
| **Fake Company Profiles** | Medium | Medium | Implement an admin verification queue. All new companies are set to `is_verified = false` and their job posts remain in draft mode until a Super Admin reviews and approves the company. |
| **Rapid Database Storage Growth**| Medium | Medium | Store large files (resumes, logos) in **Cloudflare R2** rather than the database. The database will only store R2 URLs and lightweight parsed JSON metadata. |
| **SEO Indexing Issues** | Low | High | Use Next.js SSR for all public job and company pages. Avoid client-side rendering (CSR) for indexable routes. Set up automated sitemaps and monitor Google Search Console. |

## 2. Deep Dive Mitigation Plans

### 2.1 Employer Domain Verification
To prevent malicious actors from posing as legitimate companies (e.g., posting fake jobs under "Google" using a Gmail account):
1. **Domain Match Check:** The registration system checks the employer's email domain against their company website domain. (e.g., `hr@google.com` matches `google.com`).
2. **Public Domain Block:** Common public email domains (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) are blocked from registering as employers.
3. **DNS Verification (Optional):** For high-profile companies, require the employer to add a TXT record to their DNS to prove ownership before verification.

### 2.2 Database Growth Control (Resumes & Logs)
To prevent the PostgreSQL database from slowing down due to massive binary data storage:
- **Offload Binaries:** Resumes are uploaded directly from the client to Cloudflare R2 using a secure, short-lived presigned URL generated by the Go API. The database only records the metadata.
- **Log Rotation:** Audit logs and system logs are partitioned by month. Logs older than 90 days are archived to cold storage (R2) and purged from the active database.
