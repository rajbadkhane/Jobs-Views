# Blue Collar Workflow

## Purpose

Blue-collar hiring often needs fewer interviews, stronger document checks, shift readiness, and joining coordination. Sprint 22 adds templates for these workflows without changing existing ATS APIs.

## Supported Templates

- Security Guard
- Driver
- Delivery Executive
- Warehouse Associate
- Factory Worker
- Electrician
- Plumber
- Housekeeping
- Office Boy
- Receptionist

## Common Stages

1. Applied
2. Screening
3. Shortlisted
4. HR Interview
5. Offer
6. Hired
7. Joined
8. Closed

## Common Documents

- Aadhaar
- PAN
- Driving License where required
- Police Verification where required
- Medical Fitness
- Certificates

## Joining Checklist

- Documents collected
- Background check
- Shift assigned
- Uniform or equipment issued
- Site briefing
- Attendance setup
